PerformativeInterviews

Video series · danced conversations

The performative interviews, which can also be described as danced conversations, are events that seek to create a space for exchanging knowledge through the experience of being together, arising from simple actions (organised in advance, yet open to the unpredictable) that point towards the awakening of a field of singularity and sharing.

They are encounters guided by a fluid, ever-changing method, opening closer forms of listening and contact and drawing the guest artist into an aesthetic experience, in exchanges that bring knowledge of the sensible into interaction and discussion.

Marcus Moreno has developed this practice in recent years from a proposal by Marisa Lambert, who supervised his master's in Performing Arts at Unicamp.
