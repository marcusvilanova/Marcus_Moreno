Biography

Marcus Moreno is a dance artist with a master's degree in Performing Arts from Unicamp (Brazil) and a specialization in the Klauss Vianna Technique (PUC-SP). His work moves across poetic fields to investigate temporality, improvisation in performance and the making of networks of affect.

Since 2012 he has created stage works, always in partnership with other artists. They include A Imagem como Ausência (2013), A Flor da Lua (2016), Estudo para o Encontro (2017), Instante-já (2019), created with Uruguayan artist Andrea Arobba and nominated for the APCA Dance Award, and Onde Houver Céu (2024), also nominated for the APCA Award. Since 2016 he has developed the Encontros Efêmeros (Ephemeral Encounters), improvisations with artists from the contemporary scene, and in 2022, with Marisa Lambert and Mariana Molinos, he created the Entrevistas-performativas (Performative Interviews), an audiovisual series on improvisation and affect.

As a cultural manager, he coordinated São Paulo's Municipal Dance Support Program (Fomento à Dança) and was a cultural programmer at Oficina Cultural Oswald de Andrade from 2020 to 2024. He has taken part in international arts management meetings at Festival Danzalborde (Chile), FIDCU (Uruguay) and EGA (Argentina).
